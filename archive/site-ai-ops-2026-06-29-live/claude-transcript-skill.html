What it does

You recorded something. Now turn it into content that actually gets published.

How it works

Four steps from recording to published content.

  1. Build your brand profile

    Point the skill at a folder of your brand docs — style guide, published posts, about page. It builds a reusable voice profile once. Reuse it on every run.

  2. Drop in a transcript

    Paste it, point to a file, or give it a URL. Works with raw text, VTT/SRT captions, and auto-generated transcripts. The built-in cleaner handles the mess.

  3. Get your content

    Ask for what you need — a blog post, a thread, show notes, a summary, or all of them. Every piece carries source timecodes back to the original recording.

  4. Export editor files

    FCPXML, FCP7 XML, EDL, and CSV drop straight into your timeline. Cut from the moments that matter instead of scrubbing through hours of footage.
