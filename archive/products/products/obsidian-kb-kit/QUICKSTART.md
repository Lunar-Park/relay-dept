# 🚀 Quick Start Guide

Welcome to your new knowledge base! This guide will get you up and running in 5 minutes.

## Step 1: Open the Vault

1. Open Obsidian
2. Click **Open folder as vault**
3. Select the `vault` folder from this kit
4. Done — you're in!

## Step 2: Tour the Structure

Your vault has 5 main folders:

| Folder | What Goes Here |
|--------|----------------|
| **00-Inbox** | Quick capture — dump anything here |
| **01-Journal** | Daily notes and reflections |
| **02-Projects** | Active projects with goals |
| **03-Areas** | Ongoing life areas (health, finance, etc.) |
| **04-Resources** | Reference material and reading notes |
| **05-Archive** | Completed or inactive stuff |

## Step 3: Create Your First Note

1. Go to **00-Inbox**
2. Press `Cmd+N` (Mac) or `Ctrl+N` (Windows)
3. Type anything — a thought, a link, a task
4. Don't worry about where it goes yet

## Step 4: Use Templates

When you're ready to organize:

1. Create a new note
2. Click the **Templates** icon (or press `Cmd+P` and type "template")
3. Pick a template (Daily Note, Project, Meeting, etc.)
4. Fill it in

## Step 5: Set Up Daily Notes (Optional)

1. Go to **Settings → Core Plugins → Daily notes**
2. Set the folder to `01-Journal`
3. Choose the `Daily Note` template
4. Now you can open today's note with one click

## Tips for Success

- **Start messy.** Capture first, organize later.
- **Process your inbox.** Spend 5 minutes each day moving notes to the right place.
- **Link things together.** Use `[[double brackets]]` to connect notes.
- **Review weekly.** Look at your projects, check your journal, tidy up.
- **Don't overthink it.** There's no perfect system — just use it.
