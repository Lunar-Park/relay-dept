# Reference: Video Summary

A short summary plus key moments, sized for a YouTube description, a "what you'll learn" block, or an internal recap. Tighter than show notes.

## Structure

1. **TL;DR** — 2-3 sentences capturing the whole video. This is the part most people read.
2. **What you'll learn** — 3-5 bullets, each a concrete outcome or insight ("How to…", "Why…", "The mistake most people make with…").
3. **Key moments** — 4-8 timestamped highlights (if timestamps exist):
   ```
   (01:12) The core idea
   (06:40) Live example
   (12:05) The biggest mistake to avoid
   ```
   If no timestamps, replace with a "Highlights" bullet list instead — and note timestamps weren't available.
4. **One-line CTA** — Subscribe / next video / link. Placeholder if none given.

## Variants (offer based on use case)

- **YouTube description** — TL;DR + key moments + CTA, with 3-5 relevant tags/hashtags at the end.
- **Micro-summary** — Just the TL;DR + 3 bullets, for a newsletter blurb or Slack post.
- **Chapter markers only** — Just the timestamped key moments, formatted for YouTube chapters (must start at `00:00`).

## Length

- TL;DR: ≤ 60 words
- Whole summary: ≤ 250 words

## Don't

- Don't restate the entire video — this is a teaser/recap, not a transcript.
- Don't invent timestamps or metrics.
