# Stop building features nobody asked for

Here's the trap: building feels productive. Talking to customers feels slow. So teams default to building — and end up with a roadmap full of things that sounded cool in a meeting.

We use one rule: no feature ships until three real customers describe the problem in their own words. Not the solution — the problem. Customers will ask for a faster horse; our job is to hear what's underneath.

It's uncomfortable at first. It's also the highest-leverage hour you'll spend all week.

— RelayDept, tools for people who build things
