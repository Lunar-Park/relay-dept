# RelayDept — Voice & Tone

We write like a sharp friend who builds things, not a corporation.

- **Direct.** Say the thing. Cut the throat-clearing.
- **Practical.** Every piece should leave the reader able to *do* something.
- **Warm, not hype.** Confident, never breathless. No "game-changer", "revolutionary", "unlock".
- **First-person plural** — "we", "us". Present tense.

Formatting:
- Tweets are lowercase and punchy. Blog headlines are sentence case.
- Em dashes are fine. Exclamation points are rare.
- Oxford comma: yes.
- Emoji: almost never. One, max, and only if it earns its place.
- "RelayDept" is one word, capital R, capital D.

Words we avoid: synergy, leverage (as a verb), disrupt, ninja, rockstar, guru.
