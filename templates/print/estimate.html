Contingency

Ten percent of production costs, held by Relay and released only with Limitless's written approval against a named line. Unused contingency is not invoiced.

Assumptions

  • One controlled interior location and one shoot day. The public street opening is discovery and interview material only.
  • Limitless supplies approved framing and disclaimer language and one named notes owner before pre-production lock.
  • Prize funding, custody and tax handling are the client's and are not in this estimate.
  • Rates are benchmarks; fabrication, insurance, location and any creator fee are re-quoted before the SOW.
  • English-language delivery of the deliverables in the production treatment. Raw footage and project files are not included.

Excluded

  • Production insurance certificate, permits and contest-rules review: quoted separately once the location is fixed.
  • Featured strength creator or athlete fee and usage: allowance to be agreed.
  • Media, seeding, distribution, additional versions, languages or revision rounds beyond those in the SOW.
Gate
Approval of this estimate is not authorisation to incur non-refundable costs. Production commitments begin only after the SOW is executed and the deposit or funded milestone has cleared.
